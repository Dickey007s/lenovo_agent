## Supported decisions

- The product foreground is one browsable office folder workspace, not a
  registry of pre-authored Demo or Scenario entries.
- Users can inspect file information before choosing task scope and writing an
  original instruction.
- CSV, PDF, DOCX and TXT need bounded preview, citation, integrity validation
  and explicit security behavior.
- The reporting record must connect architecture differences to user-flow and
  frontend consequences, and must retain concrete scenarios and sources.

## Limitations

This feedback comes from one project Stakeholder. It does not establish the
frequency of the problem, compare completion time, measure trust or prove that
the new folder-first interaction is better. Those claims require target-user
research and controlled task observation.
